# Fourth real-agent schema-test selection

Status: unpublished research note. No product was installed, run, contacted or
evaluated.

## Bounded candidate comparison

| Candidate | Proposed schema stress | Identity and applicability boundary | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Jules | Unresolved disagreement between two publisher sources about service lifecycle | Strong: a dated Jules changelog says the service left beta on 6 August 2025, while the current Jules FAQ says it is in Public Beta. Both name the same hosted Jules surface; the dated claim and rolling-current claim remain separately scoped. | Selected |
| Devin | Potential independent evaluation | The hosted service is identifiable, but a bounded pass did not find a primary-source-compatible public evaluation with sufficiently explicit service time, model, plan, repository, tool and task applicability. | Not selected |
| Cursor Background Agents | Potential publisher-source disagreement | The hosted surface is identifiable, but the reviewed primary pages did not expose a comparably direct contradiction with the same proposition and defensible overlap. | Not selected |

## Why Jules

Jules supplies the narrowest defensible test of the schema's unresolved
relationship path. Google's dated page titled **Jules is out of beta!** says
Jules was no longer in beta on 6 August 2025. The current Google-hosted FAQ
says Jules is currently in Public Beta. The catalog preserves both statements,
their locators and their different time applicability. It does not infer a
correction, choose a winner or silently treat the rolling FAQ as exact evidence
for the 2025 milestone.

This case also tests a hosted-agent runtime whose backend build, model routing,
task VM identity and effective configuration are unresolved. No independent
test is included: forcing a loosely applicable public benchmark into this
record would be less defensible than the publisher-source disagreement.
