## Identity and disagreement decision

The subject is the hosted Jules service at `jules.google.com`, not Jules Tools
CLI or the Jules REST API. Google's dated 6 August 2025 changelog says Jules is
no longer in beta. The current FAQ says Jules is currently in Public Beta.
These are attributable claims about the same lifecycle proposition under
applicability that may overlap; neither source supplies a correction notice or
an exact backend build. Their reciprocal contradiction therefore remains
active in the raw claim records and is exposed as unresolved in the generic
dossier.

The current getting-started page also calls Jules experimental. That label is
retained in the dossier limitations as context but is not converted into a
third lifecycle claim because it does not necessarily contradict either beta
label.
