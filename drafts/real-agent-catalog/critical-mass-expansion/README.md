# Critical-mass expansion

Status: **unpublished additive research draft**
Reviewed: **2026-08-07**

This lane completes the accepted 55-surface catalog universe by adding 39 bounded publisher-source dossiers to the existing 16 published current surfaces. It preserves the six accepted predecessor records and every prior dossier, claim, mapping and lifecycle entry unchanged.

Each new dossier admits exactly two claims: official surface identity and the documented delivery boundary. All candidate applicability gaps remain unresolved unknowns. No represented agent was installed or run, no independent evidence was admitted, and no score, ranking, recommendation or suitability calculation was added.

The two publisher-retired surfaces are retained as one historical and one discontinued record rather than being presented as current. The resulting projection contains 55 distinct surfaces, 53 current records and 61 readable records when the six accepted predecessor records are included.
